SAHAB OASIS HOTEL
Location: Muscat, Sultanate of Oman

Sahab Oasis Hotel is a fictional four-star hotel
located in Muscat, Oman.

The hotel offers 80 rooms, including:
- 40 Standard Rooms
- 25 Deluxe Rooms
- 15 Family Suites

The hotel reception operates 24 hours a day.

The hotel provides free Wi-Fi in all guest rooms
and public areas.

The hotel is located approximately 15 km from
Muscat International Airport.
